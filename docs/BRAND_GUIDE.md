# ASODS brand guide (reference)

Logo: mountain/people mark in navy + gold, wordmark "ASODS OUTSOURCING SERVICES",
tagline "People. Performance. Partnership."

## Color palette
- Navy: #0D1B2A
- Gold: #D4AF37
- Charcoal: #333333
- Off-white: #F1F2F6
- White: #FFFFFF

## Typography
Montserrat (all weights)

## Brand values
- People first — value people, build meaningful relationships
- Excellence — deliver exceptional service with integrity and professionalism
- Partnership — collaborate for long-term client success
- Integrity — honesty, transparency, ethical standards
- Innovation — embrace technology for efficient, effective solutions

## Mission
To provide innovative, reliable and cost-effective outsourcing and human capital
solutions that empower organizations to achieve their business goals.

## Vision
To be the leading outsourcing partner in Nigeria, recognized for transforming
businesses through people, performance and partnership.

## Services (for iconography/nav)
Recruitment & Staffing, Outsourced Staff Management, Payroll Management,
Training & Development, HR Consulting, Background Verification
