# ASODS Outsourcing Limited — business summary (for build context)

Full 25-chapter business plan is kept separately by the founder; this is the
condensed version relevant to building the website/portals.

## What ASODS does
Nigerian HR outsourcing, recruitment, and BPO company (Lagos). Recruits, screens,
trains, deploys, and manages skilled professionals on behalf of client organizations
— an end-to-end workforce solution, not just a recruitment agency.

## Target clients (B2B)
Commercial banks, microfinance banks, fintechs, insurance companies, manufacturing,
telecoms, logistics, retail, government contractors, SMEs.

## Services offered
1. Recruitment services (permanent hires, one-time fee)
2. Staff outsourcing (ASODS-managed employees placed at client sites, recurring fee)
3. HR consulting
4. Payroll management
5. Training & development
6. Background verification

## Revenue model
Recurring monthly management fees (outsourced staff) + one-time recruitment fees +
payroll fees + training/consulting project fees + verification fees. Recurring
revenue is the priority — it's what makes the business predictable.

## Competitive positioning
Named competitors: Workforce Group, Phillips Outsourcing, Resource Intermediaries,
ICS Outsourcing, Kimberly Ryan, StreSERT Services, Jobberman.

Known market gaps (from ASODS's own competitor analysis): slow response times,
generic service models, limited personalization, high SME fees, delayed
communication. ASODS's website/portal system exists specifically to close these
gaps — see "Differentiation this platform must deliver" in KIRO_SPEC.md.

## Brand positioning statement
"Nigeria's trusted workforce solutions partner, delivering qualified talent through
technology, professionalism, and exceptional client service."

## Recruitment workflow (mirror this in the admin pipeline UI)
Client request -> recruitment planning -> candidate sourcing -> screening/interview
-> client review -> offer & documentation -> onboarding -> deployment -> ongoing
workforce management (attendance, performance, replacement).
